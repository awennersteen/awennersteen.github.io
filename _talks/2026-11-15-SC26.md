I will be presenting a half-day tutorial at [SC26](https://sc26.supercomputing.org/) with the [Quantum Resource Management Interface (QRMI)](https://github.com/qiskit-community/qrmi):

> Write Once, Run Anywhere: Resource-Manager-Agnostic QPU Integration with QRMI


HPC centers face two kinds of fragmentation: quantum vendors expose different APIs, while sites run different job schedulers.
QRMI addresses both with open-source Python, C, and Rust interfaces for heterogeneous QPU backends and native integration with HPC resource managers.

The session will run the same QRMI job through Slurm, PBS, Open Cluster Scheduler, and Flux, targeting quantum resources from IBM, Pasqal, and Alice & Bob.
Attendees will leave with a practical understanding of how to integrate QPUs into their scheduler of choice and build portable hybrid HPC–quantum workflows.
